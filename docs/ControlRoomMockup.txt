Crusader Silencer: Level2 control-room mockup

Map: /Game/Maps/L_TraversalGym
Outliner: Crusader/Control Room
Main entrance: (20600, 5800, 130), east of the first facility.

The supplied blue control-room reference guides the U-shaped consoles,
terminals, service lockers, warning panels, crates and barrel storage. The
cross-shaped shell occupies 60 x 42 metres overall, with a 42 x 30 metre
central hall, command alcove, entry lobby and two side bays. Walls are 6.6 m
tall; entrances are 8 m wide and 5.1 m high. The roof remains on during play.
All visible building geometry comes from the 19 imported Level2 models.
Kit-panel backing closes the decorative tile openings in the floor and roof.
Metal physical materials support the existing footsteps and impact effects.

New characters: ArmoredSentinel (rifle), AstronautSentinel (pistol),
CrimsonVanguard (shotgun); MaintenanceWorker, RuggedMiner, RustboundExplorer,
CrimsonAuthority, CrusaderOperative, GraySentinel and RuggedSurvivor (unarmed).
Every model has a fitted body/finger rig and a player visual override entry.
They inherit existing human movement, avoidance, detection, combat and flight
behaviour. Each named resident is guaranteed to spawn with its own visual.

Nine connected crowd areas cover the two outside walks, entrance, lobby,
central hall, operations, command, service and storage. Reciprocal links to
Depot let existing guards, civilians and robots visit the new building.
Each new area has a 65 percent chance to choose a neighbouring area when
the existing AI considers an area change. Furniture stays outside broad routes.

Source FBXs/textures: Art/Environment/Level2/<Name>/Source and
Art/Characters/<Name>/Source. Texture files use Name_BaseColor.png,
Name_Normal.png, Name_Metallic.png and Name_Roughness.png. Original archive
bytes and names are recorded in each SourceManifest.json. Clean archives
remain under mockups; editable Blender rigs/models and FBX exports are in Art.

Author with PIE stopped through Scripts/Send-Editor.py:
  Build-ControlRoomMockup.py
  Configure-ControlRoomCrowd.py
These update only their own actor prefixes. Rebuilding the new shell resets
its managed placements; it does not rebuild the first facility.

Validation and capture scripts:
  Test-ControlRoom.py: collision, connected navigation and real player walking.
  Test-ControlRoomCrowd.py: all ten visuals/loadouts, live patrols and posture.
  Test-AdditionalNPCVisuals.py: actual widget clicks, aim, shoulders and reload.
  Capture-ControlRoom.py: human-height interiors and roof exterior.
Reports/screenshots: Artifacts/ControlRoom and Artifacts/NPCCharacters/Additions.
Durable results: resources/ControlRoomValidation.json.
