Crusader Silencer: crowd district, environment and surface audio

Open src/CrusaderSilencer.uproject and /Game/Maps/L_TraversalGym.
The crowd district connects to the east side of the original gym. It has six
guards (two rifles, two pistols, two shotguns) and six unarmed civilians.
Commons, Market, Depot and Refuge are connected navigation tethers. NPCs pick
local destinations, pause, and have a 28 percent chance at each area-change
check to visit a neighbour. Home areas, connections, radii, speeds, loadouts
and visual choices are editable on the CrowdAgent component and crowd areas.

NPCs inherit B_CRTraversalPawn. CRCrowdController drives the same Lyra ability,
inventory, equipment, GASP animation, sliding and physics components as players.
Detour crowd following provides obstacle avoidance and agent separation.
Players register as passive crowd agents so NPCs can steer around them.
AI supplies GASP's replicated walk, sprint and strafe intent: patrol uses walk
databases, pursuit/escape can sprint, and weapon readiness enables strafing.
Desired movement speed also replicates for observer trajectory prediction.
Avoidance uses a 650 cm collision query and the default velocity range multiplier
of 1; doubling that multiplier throttled clear-path movement to about 35 percent
and left the running poses out of step with the capsule. Cached crowd limits
refresh when movement speed changes, including crouch, slide and recovery.
Blocked opposing traffic can sidestep and retry its route instead of remaining
stuck or aborting merely because another agent briefly occupies the path.
Guards detect sight and hearing, investigate footsteps or unseen gunshots,
react to damage and near misses, pursue a last known position, and eventually
resume patrol after losing the threat. Combat uses ranged bursts, lateral
repositioning, available low cover, crouched pauses, shoulder changes and
reloads. A shot trace prevents intentionally firing through another pawn.
Civilians flee nearby gunfire and damage, choosing reachable positions farther
from the threat. They calm down after combat noise stops. Dead NPCs use the
shared combat ragdoll and respawn with their role and a new loadout/appearance.

Both roles can jump, slide, hurdle, mantle, climb and vault. Parkour uses the
player's obstacle checks and motion-matched montages; weapons are stowed during
the action and restored afterward. CRCrowdTraversalLink connects navmesh edges
to these actions. Four links are authored on the original gym obstacles. New
maps need navigation bounds and suitable traversal links for disconnected
ledges; agents do not teleport across arbitrary geometry.

The widget still selects the player visual, defaulting to CrimsonSentinel.
CrowdAgent owns NPC visual selection so the widget cannot replace every NPC
with the player's appearance. Visuals and crowd state replicate from authority.
Weapon grip updates wait for both the source and retargeted skeleton poses.

The footstep test pads are southeast of the firing range, near (7800,-6300).
They cover 27 physical materials. A gallery of all 33 imported environment
models is farther south, near (7100,-11600). Labels identify each model and
surface. Model archives retain their original bytes under their new names.
Editable Blender files and FBX exports are under Art/Environment. Textures use
Name_BaseColor.png, Name_Normal.png, Name_Metallic.png and Name_Roughness.png.
Blender texture references are relative. Imported meshes use Nanite and exact
static collision; make simple collision before using them as simulated bodies.

Footsteps use TWSFX_FS_Ultimate1:
https://www.fab.com/listings/2dfec8e4-075a-4893-af6d-5ca0589a2bdd
DA_Footsteps maps all 31 source categories to the 27 physical-surface profiles.
Animation foot contacts trace the ground and choose walk, run, sprint or sneak
samples. Jump and landing use their own samples; ground transition supplies a
landing when a selected animation has no notify. Contacts are debounced and
suppressed during sliding, airborne steps, ragdoll, recovery and death.
Spatial attenuation and owner concurrency limit stacked audio. Server footstep
noise also feeds guard hearing. Proxies use local animation contacts rather
than a second audio multicast.

Bullet holes use Bullet Impact VFX:
https://www.fab.com/listings/eb49911a-44bb-4114-abd5-b4124fa6f3c9
Each receiving surface selects a decal variant with varied size and rotation.
Holes attach to the hit component, fade during the last 20 seconds of their
120-second lifetime, and are capped at 96 per shooter. Unsupported receivers,
skeletal characters, misses and loose/fluid surface profiles produce no holes.
