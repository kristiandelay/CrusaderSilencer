Crusader Silencer: three-storey Mars facility

Map: /Game/Maps/L_TraversalGym
Outliner: Crusader/Three Storey Facility
Main entrance: (33000, 5200, 130), east of the existing two buildings.
The promenade joins the control-room approach along world Y=4750.

The new Level3 kit supplies all visible construction and equipment. The main
building is 66 x 54 metres with an east stair tower. All three storeys remain
enclosed during play, with 6.6-metre clear ceilings and a complete top roof.
The reference guides the loading/reactor floor, operations/server floor and
research/containment floor. Large side bays and broad cross-corridors leave
room for the third-person camera, combat and NPC avoidance.

Wall joins use matching PlainWall metal liners embedded within the decorative
wall thickness. Vertical joints, corners, tier joins and floor-depth bands
are closed independently of the source meshes' rounded frame bounds. Liners
cover the joints while retaining all 56 window centres and the door openings.
Test-Level3WallSeams.py traces 30,071 seam positions across all 51 wall spans;
it compares the original panels with the liner collision disabled, restores
the liners and checks that none of those seams remain open. Its result and
the walkthrough are tied to the generated facility manifest hash. Floor-depth
band liners finish flush with the floor so they cannot become raised door
thresholds. All 30,071 probes are closed after the fix (2,113 were open with
the liners disabled).

Occupied floor elevations are 0, 720 and 1440 cm above the building origin.
The wide switchback stairs use four shallow kit runs per storey with resting
landings. The kit's actual first/last tread heights determine placement; its
rail-inclusive bounds are not used as the walkable rise. Stair openings stay
over landing floor, and upper slabs do not cap the stairwell.

Eight invisible continuous collision ramps carry player/NPC capsules and the
camera over the visible treads. Flat collision plates connect the landings
and room floors without decorative tile seams. Invisible side guards retain
stair safety without shortening the camera boom. Detailed visible meshes
still receive weapon/grenade hits, foot IK and metal footstep traces. The
walkthrough measures capsule height along every ramp in both directions to
catch step bounce and checks all linked crowd routes after navigation rebuild.

Three guards (rifle, pistol and shotgun) and six unarmed civilians use the
existing AI, visuals, hearing, combat response and avoidance. Connected crowd
areas cover all three floors, the stairs, entrance and outside promenade.
They connect to the previous control-room patrol network.

All 74 original archive bytes are preserved under clean Name.zip filenames
in mockups/Environment/Level3 and its props folder. Untouched normalized
FBX/textures are in Art/Environment/Level3/Name/Source; editable Blender scenes
and centimetre-normalized Export/Name.fbx files are alongside them. Names and

the existing project asset catalogs. Unreal assets are under
