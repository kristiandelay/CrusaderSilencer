Crusader Silencer weapon effects and surface range

Open src/CrusaderSilencer.uproject, then /Game/Maps/L_TraversalGym.
The surface range is south of the slide lane, around X=1200, Y=-6400.
Weapon pickups are beside its marked firing line. Solid targets: concrete,
glass, metal, wood, brick, rubber, fabric, rock, ceramic and drywall.
Ground trays: dirt, sand, water, snow, ice and mud.

Assets:
  Bullet Impact VFX by Medartimus
  https://www.fab.com/listings/eb49911a-44bb-4114-abd5-b4124fa6f3c9
  Realistic Gun VFX by Hivemind
  https://www.fab.com/listings/21573c1a-e494-4194-9926-0c03f48a5563
  Pack content is preserved under /Game/BulletImpactVFX and /Game/GunFX.
  Project profiles and physical materials: /Game/Crusader/Effects.

Rifle, pistol and shotgun use separate muzzle/ejection systems and shared
surface-aware impacts, spatial sounds, bullet trails and temporary bullet
marks. Existing firing audio, recoil, damage, ammo and reload abilities remain.
Surface IDs 1-3 keep the existing Character/Concrete/Glass meanings; 4-17 add
the new surfaces. resources/WeaponSurfaces.json contains the exact asset map.

CRWeaponEffectsComponent uses committed Lyra target data. It predicts locally
once and multicasts cosmetics to observers, skipping the predicted owner.
Every pellet retains its own trace endpoint, including misses. Misses emit
trails without impact particles, sounds or marks. Trails expire at their
trace endpoint; decals fade after 15 seconds and are bounded per shooter.
Nearby shotgun impact sounds are combined to avoid overlapping nine cues.
The old B_Weapon cosmetic path is skipped when a Crusader effects profile is
active; fire audio and animation still run through the existing gameplay cues.

Rebuild after the C++ editor build, with Play stopped:
  python Scripts/Send-Editor.py Scripts/Configure-WeaponEffects.py
  python Scripts/Send-Editor.py Scripts/Create-SurfaceRange.py
The range script updates only its CR_SurfaceRange_ actors.

Validation:
  Test-WeaponEffects.py fires real abilities at all 16 targets and checks
  surface IDs, both shoulder stances, all three weapons, pellets and misses.
  Test-WeaponEffects-Network.py checks one cosmetic burst per role for host
  and client shots, with matching replicated physical surface IDs.
  Reports/captures: Artifacts/WeaponFX/.
  Durable report: resources/WeaponEffectsValidation.json.
