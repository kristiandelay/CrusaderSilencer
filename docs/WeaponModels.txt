Crusader Silencer weapon models

Current replacements
  Rifle: Black Iron Rifle
  Pistol: Futuristic Pistol
  Shotgun: Titan Breaker

Futuristic Pistol and Titan Breaker replace the original models in the existing
pistol and shotgun equipment definitions. Pick them up with E at their stations
in L_TraversalGym. Existing ammo, damage, firing, reload, and inventory behavior
remain attached to those weapon types. Their names also appear in the HUD.

Editable source
  Art/Weapons/FuturisticPistol/FuturisticPistol.blend
  Art/Weapons/TitanBreaker/TitanBreaker.blend
  Export/ in each folder contains the prepared skeletal FBX.
  Source/ contains the original geometry and Name_Type.png PBR textures.
  SourceManifest.json and mockups/AssetSources.json preserve source names/hashes.

Rig and rendering
  Futuristic Pistol: 31 cm, 14,000 triangles at LOD0.
  Titan Breaker: 68 cm, 22,000 triangles at LOD0.
  Each uses three Unreal LODs and its existing Lyra weapon animation hierarchy.
  Moving sections have separate boundary vertices to prevent stretched faces.
  Muzzle and shell-ejection sockets are fitted to the new geometry.
  Unreal assets are under /Game/Crusader/Weapons/FuturisticPistol and TitanBreaker.
  Baseline ID_Pistol/WID_Pistol and ID_Shotgun/WID_Shotgun now use these assets.
  Weapon HUD widget copies use the existing scoped CrusaderWeaponUI domain.

Rebuild (stop PIE and run the editor bridge first)
  python Scripts/Send-Editor.py Scripts/Export-PistolShotgunReferences.py
  Blender --background --python Scripts/Prepare-PistolShotgun.py
  python Scripts/Send-Editor.py Scripts/Import-PistolShotgun.py

Validation
  Test-PistolShotgun.py: standalone PIE, both weapons and both shoulders,
    multiple aim angles, material/scale, firing, reload, and moving rig parts.
  Capture-WeaponReplacements.py: close-up grip captures after the standalone test.
  Test-PistolShotgun-Network.py: two-player listen-server PIE with Crimson Sentinel.
  resources/WeaponModelValidation.json records the results and final file hashes.
